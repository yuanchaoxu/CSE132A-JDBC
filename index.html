About AYOTEE

AYOTEE is committed to delivering high-quality, practical, and more environmentally conscious home cleaning essentials. Built around the idea of being more convenient, more reliable, and closer to real daily life, our brand develops trash bag solutions across multiple sizes, capacities, and usage scenarios for households, offices, and pet cleanup needs.

We believe great daily-use products should do more than basic functionality. They should balance strength, convenience, and environmental responsibility. AYOTEE continues to optimize product design so every trash bag replacement is simpler and more worry-free.
